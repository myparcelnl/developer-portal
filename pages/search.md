Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on a Mac or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> on Windows and Linux to open the search. You can also press <kbd>/</kbd> on any page, or use the search field in the navigation bar.

The search covers the guides, the platform manuals and every endpoint in the API reference.

## Where to start

- [Getting started](guides/getting-started.md): get your API key and send your first shipment.
- [API reference](api/README.md): every endpoint, parameter and schema.
- [Integrations](integrations.md): plug-ins, SDKs and other integrations.
